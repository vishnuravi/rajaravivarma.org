Twelve paintings

Ravi Varma painted more than two thousand canvases in his lifetime. These twelve span his range: the epics from the Mahabharata and Ramayana, the goddesses who left his press for every Indian wall, the family portraits, and the breakthrough Shakuntala that made his name at Vienna in 1873.
